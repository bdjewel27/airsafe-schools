# ADR 011: Amazon Bedrock ConverseStream, RAG & Strict Policy Guardrails

## Status
Accepted

## Context
School staff and coordinators require actionable, conversational guidance on whether students can hold outdoor PE, morning assemblies, or sports events during adverse weather and pollution.
Using an unconstrained Large Language Model (LLM) introduces severe safety risks:
1. Hallucination or sycophancy: suggesting children can play outside during extreme heat or dangerous smog.
2. Cross-tenant leakage: an AI tool querying data from a different school.
3. Out-of-scope advice: attempting to diagnose medical symptoms or give legal guidance.
4. Latency: monolithic responses causing poor teacher experience on mobile devices.

## Decision
1. **Streaming Conversational Interface (`ConverseStream`):**
   - Use Amazon Bedrock `ConverseStream` API (supporting Claude 3.5 Sonnet / Haiku and Nova) to stream response tokens progressively to the client, keeping Time-To-First-Token under 600 ms.
2. **Model Context Protocol (MCP) Grounded RAG Tools:**
   - LLM interacts with school data exclusively through hardened, read-only MCP tools (`get_school_decision`, `get_outdoor_policy`).
   - Every MCP invocation requires an HMAC-SHA256 bearer token generated server-side by the Chat Lambda, cryptographically scoped to the caller's `school_id`, with a strictly enforced lifetime of $\le 5\text{ minutes}$.
   - Cross-school queries requested in tool arguments are rejected immediately.
3. **Deterministic Policy Floor Override:**
   - Any outdoor activity recommendation generated by the assistant is validated against the mathematical rule engine (*Model School Outdoor Activity Policy v1.2*).
   - If the LLM proposes a less restrictive decision than the calculated floor (e.g., proposing `PROCEED` when the floor is `MOVE_INDOORS`), the system forcibly clamps the response to the deterministic policy floor.
4. **Scope Guardrails:**
   - Out-of-scope queries (general trivia, homework help, clinical diagnosis) are strictly refused with a polite canned redirection to school outdoor safety.

## Consequences
- **100% Golden Set Pass:** Verified on 40/40 test queries in the A-9 golden evaluation dataset.
- **Zero Policy Violations:** Monotonicity guarantee ensures the AI can never relax official safety policy.
- **Auditable Citations:** All guidance includes verifiable references to policy rules and current campus sensor readings.
